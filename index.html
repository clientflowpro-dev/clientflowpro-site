Questions clients ask

Is it actually passive?

Mostly. We do the daily work. You keep your card active and paid on time, answer the occasional question, and read your monthly report.

What do I have to do?

Help us set up (store, documents, a payment card), keep that card in good standing, and check your statement each month. Everything else is on us.

Why do you need me and my store?

eBay limits how many stores one person can run, so each store belongs to a real owner. The store stays in your name and you can see everything in it.

Who owns the store and the logins?

You do. Your eBay store, email and 2-step codes stay yours. We connect through eBay’s official sign-in, and you can disconnect us any time.

How much will I make?

We don’t promise income. Results depend on the store, the market and time. Your report shows exactly what was earned, from verified records.

Are there monthly fees?

No monthly tool fees. We pay the listing tool and the eBay store subscription ourselves and take them out of the store’s profit before the split, so they never go on your card. In a month where profit doesn’t cover them, we cover the difference.

How is my share worked out?

Sales minus eBay fees, product cost, refunds and agreed tools = verified profit. That is split at the percentage in your agreement. Cashback on purchases is 100% yours.

Will this hurt my credit?

We keep card use well under safe limits, pay down steadily and plan around your bill dates. No one can promise a bank won’t act, but our rules are built to avoid the problems that hurt credit.

What about payout holds?

New eBay stores often have payouts held for a while. We plan buying around holds, and your statement shows money on hold separately.

Do packages come to my house?

Orders ship straight to the buyer. Some returns may come back to you; we give you a free drop-off label and the cost shows on your statement.

What about taxes?

Your report and year-end summary show every number your accountant needs, and you can send them a read-only link. Not tax advice; your accountant decides.

Can I stop?

Yes. There’s an off-boarding checklist: final statement, your data, and a clean hand-back of your store.

Is this a scam?

Fair question. You keep control of your store and money, every dollar is shown with the orders behind it, and you can ask for a sample report before you sign anything.
